## How to Contribute

Because GitHub does not (and should not) allow direct public pushing to a repository, the safe and standard way to contribute is through **Pull Requests**:

### Step 1: Fork the Repository
Click the **"Fork"** button at the top-right of this repository page. This creates your own copy.

### Step 2: Make Your Changes
- Clone your fork
- Add your research, expand existing content, correct errors, or add new sources
- Commit your changes with a clear message

### Step 3: Submit a Pull Request
- Push to your fork
- Open a **Pull Request** back to this repository
- Describe what you added or changed

The maintainer will review and merge valuable contributions.

## What You Can Contribute

- 📚 **Additional analysis** — deeper examination of the transcript
- 🔍 **Source verification** — confirming mission details, astronaut names, dates
- 📊 **Comparative data** — real NASA mission transcripts for comparison
- 🌍 **Context** — information about the video source, mission background
- ✏️ **Corrections** — fixing errors in the analysis
- 💡 **New findings** — additional contradictions or observations
- 🔗 **Supporting references** — academic sources, technical documentation
- 📝 **Clarity improvements** — making the analysis easier to understand

---

## Ground Rules

1. **Respect original authorship** — TrueSiddiqui remains the research originator
2. **No monetization** — no one may sell or make money from this research or anything derived from it. Companies and platforms may use it, but may not profit from it
3. **Cite your sources** — back up new claims with references
4. **Maintain objectivity** — separate provable facts from assumptions
5. **Be constructive** — this is collaborative research building
6. **Quality over quantity** — meaningful additions are valued

---

## Analysis Standards

When contributing additional analysis:

- **Tier 1 findings** — Must be provable from the transcript itself (internal contradictions)
- **Tier 2 findings** — Wording/transcription issues visible in the text
- **Tier 3 observations** — Clearly label anything requiring external assumptions or reference data

This separation maintains the objectivity and credibility of the research.

---

## Note on Style

This research prioritizes **substance over style**. Minor spelling mistakes, typos, and formatting inconsistencies are not important. Focus on ideas, accuracy, and expanding the analysis.
